# Privacy Policy for RPS Arena

RPS Arena stores Telegram user IDs, public display names, usernames, match results, chosen weapons, scores, and game statistics.

This data is used only to run Rock Paper Scissors matches, display results, maintain player stats, and show the leaderboard.

The bot does not collect phone numbers, email addresses, passwords, payment information, or private messages outside game interactions. It does not sell or share player data with third parties.

To request deletion of your statistics, open an issue at:
https://github.com/AGNI-911-69/RPS-Arena-Bot/issues
